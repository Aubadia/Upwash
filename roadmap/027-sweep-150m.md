## S1 — LR × Muon Beta

The other optimizer settings were AdamW beta1 0.85, beta2 0.95 and
multiplier 1.5.

Final `eval/loss` (“NaN” means the run diverged to NaN):

| lr \ Muon beta | 0.9 | 0.95 | 0.98 | 0.99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.002 | 2.978 | 2.980 | 3.055 | 3.264 |
| 0.004 | **2.961** | 2.974 | 4.597 | 4.664 |
| 0.008 | 4.499 | 4.443 | 5.233 | 5.401 |
| 0.016 | 4.459 | 5.620 | 5.329 | NaN |
| 0.032 | 6.607 | 9.012 | NaN | NaN |

- The best setting is lr 0.004 with Muon beta 0.9. It is not on the grid
  edge, so no extension wave was run.
- Between 0.004 and 0.008 the loss falls off a cliff; it is not a smooth
  U-shape. Most failing runs plateau at 4.4–6 from the first eval onward
  (10% of the run). Two failed later:
  - lr 0.008, Muon beta 0.9 reached an eval loss of 3.50 at 30% of the run.
    It drifted up from 40%, train loss spiked at about 61%, and the eval loss
    peaked at 5.0.
  - lr 0.032, Muon beta 0.95 jumped from 6.8 to 8.8 at about 69%.
- Higher Muon beta lowers the stable LR: at Muon beta 0.98 and 0.99, lr 0.004
  already fails.
- The optimum sits just below the cliff. The cliff's exact position between
  0.004 and 0.008 is unmeasured.

## S3 — AdamW beta2 × LR Multiplier

All S3 runs used lr 0.004, Muon beta 0.9 and beta1 0.9.

| beta2 \ multiplier | 1.5 | 1.6 | 1.7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.95 | 2.9592 | 2.9621 | 2.9570 |
| 0.98 | 2.9428 | 2.9425 | 2.9428 |
| 0.99 | 2.9399 | **2.9356** | 2.9409 |

- beta2 matters. Going from 0.95 to 0.98 gains about 0.017, and 0.99 gains
  another 0.003–0.007. Both gains are at or above the seed noise. 0.99 is
  the grid edge, so a higher beta2 (for example 0.995) has not been tested.
- The multiplier is flat within about 0.005, comparable to the noise. 1.6 is
  the best value at beta2 0.99 only.
- Current best: lr 0.004, Muon beta 0.9, beta1 0.9, beta2 0.99,
  multiplier 1.6, eval/loss 2.9356 (PPL 18.83).

## Driver Incident

At 07:55 the driver stopped on `s1-lr0.032-mb0.98` (job 773894). The job had
exited 0, but its summary was `nan`, which the summary regex did not match.
The driver therefore classified the run as a code error. The fix: a NaN run is
now a valid result with `eval/loss = inf` and `diverged: true`. The stop flag
was cleared, and the driver restarted at 07:59 and resumed. Backups:
`sweep_driver.py.bak-0800` and `state.json.bak-0800`.
